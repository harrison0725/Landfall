# 3. Flooding
* **How deep:** deepest water **11.56 ft** above ground; **70 buildings** had water around them. Water surface set at **10.9 ft NAVD88**.
* **How measured:** ground elevation vs water surface; checked against **USGS HWM 46592 (10.96 ft, 29.13589, -83.029776 - well defined seed line inside women's bathroom)** and **NOAA gauge 8727520 (10.843 ft, 29.135, -83.0317 peak 2024-09-27 04:42 UTC)**. Post-storm photos: **NOAA flight 20240928b-rgb**; pre-storm: **USDA NAIP 2023-02-15**. Flood depth per building from surveyed surface minus LiDAR ground.
* **Why undercounted:** Aerial photos cannot see inside a house or first-floor height. Where water stood around a house but height is unknown, the draft says **affected**; the real PDA category may be higher (Major if ≥18 in. inside). In this area **46 affected + 2 minor drafts have ≥1.5 ft of water** and need a field check — they are the most likely to be upgraded.
