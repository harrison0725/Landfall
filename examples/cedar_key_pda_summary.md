**DRAFT - Preliminary Damage Assessment - Cedar Key - For trained assessor review only - Not an official determination**

### 1. Headline
Hurricane surge inundated Cedar Key on 2024-09-27 (peak water surface 10.9 ft NAVD88) with post-storm NOAA flight 20240928b-rgb showing widespread flood effects. Of 150 buildings assessed, 48 have draft damage (102 none, 37 affected, 4 minor, 2 major, 5 destroyed) and 66 drafts are flagged for human field check; deepest surveyed water was 11.56 ft above ground.

### 3. Flooding
- **How deep:** 70 of 150 buildings had water around them. 39 of 48 damaged drafts had ≥1.5 ft; deepest was 11.56 ft at b145 (29.13974, -83.03506). Other deep examples: b087 10.61 ft, b139 9.46 ft, b114 7.68 ft, b105 7.63 ft.
- **How measured:** Flood depth = modeled peak water surface (10.9 ft NAVD88, tied to USGS HWM 46592 at 29.13589, -83.029776 elev 10.96 ft - "Well defined Seed line/waterline inside women's bathroom" and NOAA gauge 8727520 at 29.135, -83.0317 elev 10.843 ft peak 2024-09-27 04:42 UTC) minus ground elevation at the structure. Pre-storm USDA NAIP 2023-02-15 vs post-storm NOAA 20240928b-rgb.
- **Why flood-only houses may be undercounted:** Aerial nadir photos cannot see inside a house or measure first-floor height / pilings. Where water stood around a house but first-floor height is unknown, the draft defaults to **affected** based on visible roof/wall condition; interior flooding may be 18 in.+ and the true PDA category may be Major or higher. Example: b145 has intact roof (drafted affected, needs review) but 11.56 ft of water at ground; b139 (affected, 9.46 ft), b073 (affected, 6.65 ft), b005 (major, 6.79 ft) all depend on field verification of interior waterline.

### 6. Limits - what aerial drafts can't see
- **Inside the house:** Aerial nadir photos cannot see interior waterlines, first-floor elevation, pilings height, foundation failure under roof, or contents damage. Where water stood around a house but first-floor height is unknown, the draft says **affected**; the real PDA category may be higher (Minor/Major/Destroyed) - this is flagged as needs_review.
- **Roof condition:** Small openings, lifted shingles, tarps, and shadow/angle artifacts are hard to distinguish. Confidence for Major drafts b005 (0.55) and b087 (0.60) is low.
- **Structure loss metric:** Segment Anything "not found" % is alignment-sensitive; neighbors-based alignment used for b098/b023. Ground truth requires field photos at oblique angle and interior high-water marks.
- **These are drafts for trained assessors, not official FEMA determinations.** All 48 damaged drafts and especially the 43 flagged affected/minor/major plus flood-only affected houses (30 with ≥1.5 ft water alone) must be field-verified before counts are forwarded as official PDA. Do not share building-level drafts outside assessor channels without this disclaimer.
